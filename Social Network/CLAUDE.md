# BUHO VISION - SOCIAL NETWORK MEMORY

## PROJECT OVERVIEW
Content distribution and social media management system for Buho Vision sports videos - automating publishing across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms.

## CONTENT PIPELINE
1. **Source Content**: Full game recordings from Automation Process
2. **Derivatives**: Highlights, clips, thumbnails, social posts
3. **Distribution**: Multi-platform publishing
4. **Analytics**: Performance tracking and optimization

## PHASE 1: YOUTUBE OPTIMIZATION (PLANNED)
### Requirements
- Automated thumbnail generation
- SEO-optimized titles and descriptions
- Consistent tagging strategy
- Playlist management
- Schedule publishing

### Content Strategy
- Full games → Main channel
- Highlights → Shorts/Reels
- Key moments → Social clips

## PHASE 2: SOCIAL MEDIA EXPANSION (FUTURE)
### Instagram
- Match highlights (60s max)
- Score graphics as posts
- Stories for live updates

### TikTok
- Goal compilations
- Best plays
- Viral moments

### Twitter/X
- Final scores
- Key statistics
- Thread summaries

## PLATFORM SPECIFICATIONS
### YouTube
- Video: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- Thumbnail: 1280x720
- Max title: 100 characters
- Max description: 5000 characters

### Instagram
- Feed: 1080x1080 (square)
- Reels: 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Stories: 1080x1920
- Max video: 60 seconds (feed), 90 seconds (reels)

### TikTok
- Video: 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Max length: 10 minutes
- Optimal: 15-60 seconds
