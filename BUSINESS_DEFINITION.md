# 🦉 BUHO VISION - BUSINESS DEFINITION

## 🎯 COMPANY OVERVIEW
**Buho Vision** is a sports video production and technology company based in Uruguay, specializing in automated game recording and video distribution for amateur and semi-professional sports leagues.

---

## 🏢 BUSINESS MODEL

### 1️⃣ **Recording as a Service (RaaS)**
- **What**: Full-service game recording for sports leagues
- **How**: Using Veo.co automated cameras (no cameraman needed)
- **Coverage**: Entire leagues, all games of the season
- **Sports**:
  - Football/Soccer (95% - primary focus)
  - Basketball
  - American Football
  - Rugby
- **Market**: Amateur and semi-professional leagues in Uruguay
- **Deliverables**:
  - Full game recordings uploaded to Veo.co platform
  - Edited videos with score graphics on YouTube
  - Professional video production without cameraman costs

### 2️⃣ **Camera Hardware Sales**
- **Product**: Veo.co automated camera systems
- **Target Customers**:
  - Video analysts
  - Player scouts
  - Teams for training analysis
  - Coaches for tactical review
  - Talent recruiters
- **Value Proposition**: AI-powered cameras that track action automatically

## 📊 CURRENT OPERATIONS

### Workflow Pipeline
```
1. PRE-GAME
   ↓
2. RECORDING (Veo Camera - Automated)
   ↓
3. UPLOAD (to Veo.co platform)
   ↓
4. DOWNLOAD (from Veo.co)
   ↓
5. GRAPHICS (Score overlays - NOW AUTOMATED ✅)
   ↓
6. EDITING (Premiere Pro)
   ↓
7. EXPORT (Final video)
   ↓
8. DISTRIBUTION (YouTube)
   ↓
9. SOCIAL MEDIA (Clips & highlights)
```
